Unit 16 talks · about 5–6 hours

Culture, Tradition & Progress

How do traditions survive in a changing world? How does culture shape the way we behave? You will listen to six speakers from Afghanistan, Australia, Norway, the Gulf, Sweden and Sudan.

By the end of this unit

  • I can predict the content of a talk from its title and opening.
  • I can identify the main idea of a talk on the first listen.
  • I can use signpost language to follow the organization of a talk.
  • I can tell the main points from the examples that support them.
  • I can take short notes with key words, abbreviations and symbols.

Strategies in this unit

  1. S1Predict before you listen. Use the title, pictures and what you already know to guess what you will hear.
  2. S2Listen for the main idea (gist). On the first listen, follow the big message. Don't stop for every word.
  3. S3Recognize signpost language. Notice the words that tell you where the talk is going.
  4. S4Listen for examples and supporting details. Tell the difference between a main point and the examples that support it.
  5. S5Take notes with key words and symbols. Write only the important words. Use abbreviations and symbols to save time.
