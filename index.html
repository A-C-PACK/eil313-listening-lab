EIL313 · Academic listening · CEFR B1

Learn to listen like a university student.

18 real TED talks. 16 listening strategies, one step at a time. Each lesson shows you how to listen, not just what to answer.

Start Lesson 1.1How it works

  • Learn a strategy

    Each lesson teaches one listening strategy with a real example from the talk.

  • Listen three times

    First for the main idea, then for the strategy task, then to check and train your ear.

  • Use what you heard

    Learn academic words, write a summary from your notes, and prepare for class discussion.
