S15

Strategy 15 · Unit 3

Summarize and paraphrase from notes

Use only your notes to explain the talk in your own words.

What is it?

A summary gives the speaker's main idea and key supporting points in a few sentences. Paraphrasing means saying it in your own words, not copying the speaker's sentences.

Why does it help?

Summarizing shows you really understood. In university, you summarize lectures for exams, essays and discussions.

How to do it

  1. Look only at your notes, not the transcript.
  2. Start with the speaker and the main idea: "In this talk, X argues that…"
  3. Add 2–3 key points or examples. Leave out small details.
  4. Use your own words. Change the sentence structure, not just single words.

Listen for

Summary language
  • In this talk, X argues / explains that…
  • According to X,…
  • X gives the example of…
  • X's main point is that…
  • Overall,…
Reporting verbs
  • argues
  • explains
  • describes
  • claims
  • suggests
  • shows

Tip: If you can't write the summary from your notes, the notes need improving. That is useful information too.

Also use: S12 Follow problem–solution structure · S14 Take comparison notes (T-chart)

Exam practice: beyond the words

On the final exam, many questions ask what a speaker means or why they say something. The answer is not in the words alone: think about what comes before and after.

Watch out for these wrong answers: ideas the speaker did not say; answers that are too strong (“all”, “always”, “only”); the opposite of the speaker's view; and answers with the right words but the wrong meaning.

Show a model summary

Alejandro Aravena argues that design's "power of synthesis" can solve complex city problems with simple solutions, especially when communities take part. First, his team worked with 100 poor families in Iquique and built "half of a good house", which the families then expanded into middle-class homes. Second, they designed an office building that turned the typical glass tower inside out, reducing energy use for cooling by two thirds through common sense, not technology. Finally, after the 2010 tsunami, the community helped design a forest that protects the city of Constitución from waves and floods and gives people access to the river. By combining three government projects, they even saved four million dollars.

112 words. Your summary can be different. Compare the content, not the exact words.
